# Who gets to stay cool? Surface heat and social inequality in the Ruhr

Maria Boudjelal, independent researcher, Algiers, Algeria (ORCID 0009-0002-6434-784X)

Status (November 2026): set-up and novelty check. The analysis plan will be preregistered on OSF before any heat and census data are combined.

**Question.** In the Ruhr (Germany), are the 100 m census grid cells with lower rents, more foreign residents, more older people or more children hotter on summer days, as measured by Landsat land surface temperature, and how much of that gap goes with tree cover and sealed surfaces?

**Data (planned).** Zensus 2022 100 m grid (Destatis, dl-de/by-2-0); Landsat 8 and 9 Collection 2 Level 2 surface temperature (USGS); Copernicus Land Monitoring Service layers (tree cover density, imperviousness, Urban Atlas). Full attribution lines will be added when the data are used.

**Layout.** `00_setup/` data inventory and novelty check, `00_protocol/` preregistration, `01_data/` to `04_grid_join/` data processing, `05_analysis/` models and results, `verification/` independent checks, `VERIFICATION_LOG.md` every check run.
